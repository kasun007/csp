Our mission and values

What we do

CSP promotes philosophical education and discussion among people from different backgrounds through courses, lectures, workshops, and community conversations. Our programs range from introductory sessions for complete newcomers to focused workshops on specific thinkers and ideas.

Why it matters

Sri Lanka is home to rich and diverse intellectual traditions, but formal philosophical education is often limited to a small number of academic institutions. CSP works to close that gap — offering programs in English and Sinhala so that language is never a barrier to engaging with philosophy.
