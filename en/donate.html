Why it matters

A non-profit, by design

Officially registered under the Societies Ordinance, the Colombo School of Philosophy is a non-profit organization working toward social progress. The financial contributions and support of donors are essential to successfully carry out the programmes run by our institute — study circles, publications, seminars, and educational video productions.
